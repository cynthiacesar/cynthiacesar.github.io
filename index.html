Digital Logic

BCD 24-Hour Digital Clock

A fully synchronous 24-hour clock designed and built from discrete logic chips, with no microcontroller.

  • Counts 00:00:00 to 23:59:59 from a 32.768 kHz crystal oscillator, CD4060 and cascaded CD4518 counters.
  • Added combinational reset logic for 60-second, 60-minute and 24-hour rollover, plus a gated time-set control.
  • Moved from breadboard to a soldered perf-board build; PCB and enclosure design are planned next.
